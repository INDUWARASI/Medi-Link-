# MediLink 🏥💙
**Care for Generations**

MediLink is a comprehensive, dual-interface mobile application built with Flutter, designed to seamlessly bridge the gap between patients managing their medications and caregivers monitoring their health. 

Whether you are an individual keeping track of complex daily pill schedules or a caregiver responsible for the well-being of multiple family members, MediLink offers an intuitive, modern, and reliable solution to ensure no dose is ever missed.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 👤 For Patients
- **Personalized Schedule:** View your daily medications organized intuitively by Morning, Afternoon, and Night.
- **Smart Reminders:** Timely alerts ensure you take your medications on schedule.
- **Health Reports:** Automatic tracking of adherence and health metrics that can be easily exported for your doctor.
- **Vibrant & Friendly UI:** A premium, modern interface with smooth animations and clear typography (Google Fonts Inter) that makes tracking health engaging rather than a chore.

### 👥 For Caregivers
- **Priority Alerts:** Get instant notifications if a patient misses their scheduled medication dose.
- **Patient Overview Center:** Monitor multiple patients from a single dashboard, complete with status tags (e.g., Routine, Urgent Review).
- **Remote Adjustments:** Remotely add or modify prescription schedules for patients directly from the app.
- **Actionable Insights:** Single-tap actions to call the patient, send a reminder ping, or acknowledge an alert.

## 🎨 UI & UX Design
This app was built with a strong focus on **Visual Excellence**:
- **Color Palette:** Features a trustworthy deep teal (`#006B70`), combined with energetic corals, gentle cyans, and clean white backgrounds.
- **Typography:** Powered by `Google Fonts (Inter)` for high readability and a premium feel.
- **Experience:** Floating Action Buttons (FAB), custom Bottom Navigation, and visually distinct cards keep the workflow frictionless.
